§ 01 — Introduction

Three words. Slave Of Allah.

That's the whole bio — and the whole job description.

No niche branding, no personal mission statement longer than the message itself. The feed is what it says it is: short Islamic reminders, posted when they're ready, across the platforms where people happen to be.

Started October 2020. Four years in, the posts are still short, still reminders — alhamdulillah.
